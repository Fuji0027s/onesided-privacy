Privacy Policy

Effective: October 23, 2026 · Last updated: October 23, 2026

  • Your data export file is analyzed 100% on your device. It is never uploaded.
  • We ask for your consent before the first file is opened, and you can withdraw it at any time.

  • The only network activity is in-app purchase processing via Apple / Google and RevenueCat. Your connection lists are never sent to them.

1. Consent Before Processing (Thailand PDPA / GDPR)

Before OneSided reads any data export file for the first time, the app shows a Data Privacy Notice explaining what is read, where it is processed, and what is stored. Processing only begins after you tick the consent checkbox and tap "Agree & Choose File".

  • Legal basis: your explicit consent, under the Thailand Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (PDPA) and Article 6(1)(a) of the EU GDPR.
  • If you decline: no file is opened or read. You can still use the built-in demo dataset, which contains no personal data.
  • Withdrawing consent: tap "Erase All Local Data" at the bottom of the app. This deletes all stored scan results, your Safe List, and your consent record (purchased keys are preserved on your device so your paid purchases are not lost).

3. Exporting Your Results (CSV)

  • Before a file is created, a confirmation screen shows how many accounts will be exported and reminds you that anyone you share it with can see the usernames.
  • On iOS / Android the file is written to the app's temporary cache, opened in the system share sheet so you choose the destination, and deleted when the share sheet closes.
  • The CSV contains: username, public profile URL, follow date (if present), category, and whether the account newly unfollowed you.
  • Once you share the file, its handling is governed by the app or service you sent it to.

4. In-App Purchases (Third-Party Processors)

  • Apple App Store / Google Play handle payment. We never see your card details, name, or billing address.
  • RevenueCat, Inc. verifies purchase receipts on our behalf. It receives an anonymous app user ID generated on your device, your purchase history for this app, your store country/currency, and basic device information (OS and app version). See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

Your follower/following data and export files are never sent to Apple, Google, or RevenueCat. Purchase data is not used for advertising or tracking.

5. Your Rights

Under the PDPA and GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. Because your connection data is stored only on your device, you can exercise these rights directly in the app:

  • Access / portability: use Export as CSV.
  • Erasure / withdraw consent: use "Erase All Local Data", or uninstall the app.

For purchase-related data or any other request, email onesided.app@gmail.com. You may also complain to the Office of the Personal Data Protection Committee (Thailand) or your local data protection authority.

6. Lawful Use of Your Own Data Export

OneSided only works with official data export archives that you download from your own account and select with your device's file picker, consistent with your right to data portability under GDPR Article 20, PDPA Section 31, and the CCPA. OneSided does not log in to, query, or scrape any social media platform. "Open Profile" simply opens a standard https:// link.

7. Children

OneSided is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and does not knowingly process their data.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we change how data is handled, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date. For material changes, the app will show the Data Privacy Notice again and ask for consent before processing any new file.
